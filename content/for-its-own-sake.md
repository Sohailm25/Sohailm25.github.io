Title: For Its Own Sake
Date: 2026-02-13 10:00
Category: Poems
Slug: for-its-own-sake
URL: writings/for-its-own-sake/
Save_as: writings/for-its-own-sake/index.html
Summary: On writing, presence, and reclaiming the moment.

<p class="poem">
Writing without a broader goal of content or productivity.<br>
Writing for the inherent benefit of what it provides.<br>
The act itself, more than the output.<br>
Introspection purely out of curiosity.
</p>

<p class="poem">
I need more nature time, more physical activity,<br>
more spiritual time.<br>
Time to enjoy with my wife.<br>
Time to enjoy time itself.
</p>

<p class="poem">
Self-identity lives HERE, in this moment,<br>
away from the modifiers of reality.<br>
Away from the augmentation.<br>
Purely for its own sake.
</p>

<p class="poem">
Spirituality and nature should be purely for their own sake.<br>
Not arenas to anything<br>
but to be present in the current moment.<br>
A refresh.
</p>

<p class="poem">
This is what enjoying the current moment feels like<br>
with no impending task or goal or commitment<br>
or mental lease that jobs, people, our phones steal from us.
</p>

<p class="poem">
The irony is I already think about taking a picture<br>
and posting about this.<br>
But for a moment this was MINE,<br>
it belongs to me.
</p>

<p class="poem">
No outside force manipulating the reality of my mind<br>
before it can hit the paper.
</p>

<p class="poem">
This is when I imagine kids, a ranch, animals—<br>
all would be a force multiplier.<br>
I already have my wife and our kittens.<br>
Savor the moment.
</p>

<p class="poem">
High highs. Low lows. Mad scientist.<br>
Purpose of our lives.
</p>

<p class="poem">
Goals and aspirations are an anchor direction<br>
but not the vessel itself.
</p>

<p class="poem">
Why do I fear old age?<br>
Why do I feel my life won't be meaningful<br>
or amount to being useful then?
</p>
