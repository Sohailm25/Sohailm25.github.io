Research question and why it matters

Goal split

We intentionally split two goals: (1) behavior-level decision validity and (2) mechanism-level specificity. This avoids overclaiming when output control works but internal evidence is still weak.See canonical claim envelope and end-state artifacts for enforced claim boundaries.

Community value

This case shows a stricter standard beyond necessary/sufficient tests: include leakage risk, alternative-cause checks, and hard stop rules before mechanism claims.

Beyond necessary and sufficient: practical validity ladder

  1. Behavioral effect: Did the intervention move behavior?
  2. Decision validity: Are measurements trustworthy and reproducible?
  3. Selectivity/leakage checks: Did unrelated behavior move too?
  4. Alternative-cause checks (X′): Could another factor explain the result?
  5. Mechanistic specificity: Can internal explanation quality clear gates?
