## How to add videos

1. Open your Loom video → Share → Embed
2. Copy the embed code
3. Add it here with a heading above it:

```
## Video Title

<div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.25%; height: 0; margin-bottom: 2em;">
  <iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/YOUR_VIDEO_ID" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;"></iframe>
</div>
```

Replace `YOUR_VIDEO_ID` with your actual video ID from the Loom share link.
