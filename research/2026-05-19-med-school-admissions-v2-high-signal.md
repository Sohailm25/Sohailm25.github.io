## §2 — The four "calling-card" phrases the 2025–2026 cycle is reading for

These phrases recur in every ADCOM commentary post-COVID and post-SFFA. Their presence on a clinical-applicant site = positive scan. Their absence = neutral. Their *opposites* = negative.

1. **Longitudinal patient relationships** / **longitudinal care** / **longitudinal continuity** — favored over "I saw a lot of patients." Maha's 3-year tenure with the same chronic-disease patients on biologics is *exactly* this. ([Michigan Medical School curriculum](https://medschool.umich.edu/programs-admissions/md-program/md-curriculum/longitudinal-learning), [Inspira non-trad guide](https://www.inspiraadvantage.com/blog/non-traditional-medical-applicant))

2. **Interprofessional collaboration** / **interprofessional team** — explicit AAMC + accreditor priority since ~2018, intensified since 2022. Lead MA + scribe roles literally are interprofessional team experience. ([Michigan IPE](https://medschool.umich.edu/programs-admissions/md-program/md-curriculum/longitudinal-learning/longitudinal-learning-interprofessional-education))

3. **Patient-centered care** — broadest priority for the cycle. Frame the Mahaclinic tool as a patient-centered intervention (not as "I built a tool"). ([AcceptMed 2025–2026 trends](https://www.acceptmed.com/blog/what-medical-schools-want-to-see-in-2025-2026-updated-priorities-trends))

4. **Systems-based practice** — ACGME core competency; appears in AAMC core EPAs; relevant for any tool that addresses a clinical workflow gap. Mahaclinic IS systems-based practice in the proper sense. ([ACGME systems-based practice](https://www.acgme.org/) — general framework, not a specific URL)

**Action for the site:** edit the About essay, the case study, and the Pediatric Scribe experience row to use these four phrases naturally. Don't keyword-stuff. One placement per phrase, in context.

## §3 — TMDSAS-specific framing (Texas schools — UT Southwestern, UT Houston, TCU, Dell, Texas A&M, UT Tyler, Sam Houston, UNT-HSC TCOM)

The TMDSAS Personal Characteristics Essay prompt asks you to *"describe your personal qualities, characteristics, skills, or strengths, and how they will contribute meaningfully to the lives of others"* ([TMDSAS application guide](https://www.tmdsas.com/application-guide/essays.html); 2,500 characters incl. spaces).

The framing the prompt rewards:
- **"Contribute meaningfully"** — the prompt's own phrase. Echo it in copy.
- **"Personal characteristics"** demonstrated through specific patient encounters, not generic claims.
- **Mission-fit language** for each school — TCU = "Empathetic Scholar," Tulane = "long-road" applicants, UTSW = research/clinical integration, A&M = primary-care, MD/DO + community health (more in v1 brief).

**Action for the site:** the About page should end with one sentence that uses "contribute meaningfully" or its echo. The competencies page is already TMDSAS-resonant.

## §4 — The MA-trained-others angle (high-signal, under-used)

Maha trained ~10 medical assistants in her Lead MA role. The 2025–2026 commentary on what gets weight:

- **Teaching others is treated as evidence of expertise, leadership, and Commitment to Learning and Growth** ([Springboard clinical experience guide](https://www.springboard.com/blog/clinical-medical-assistant/how-to-get-clinical-experience-for-medical-school/); [Med School Insiders ADCOM evaluation](https://medschoolinsiders.com/pre-med/how-med-school-adcoms-evaluate-apps/))
- **Paid clinical leadership** (the "Lead" in Lead MA) is weighted higher than equivalent unpaid hours — *"trusted to show up, stay consistent, and function as part of a real care team"* ([MedSchoolCoach evaluation](https://www.medschoolcoach.com/how-medical-schools-evaluate-applications/))
- **Hands-on roles** (MA, EMT, phlebotomy) > shadowing — *"100 hours of direct patient care often outweighs 200 hours of observation"* (same)

**Action for the site:** mention "training ~10 medical assistants" in the **Lead MA experience row** (currently buried). Add a short phrase in the Bio. Add a competency-page bullet on **Teamwork & Collaboration** about teaching peers.

## §5 — Anti-patterns to remove (every one of these is a negative scan)

| Don't write | Why |
|---|---|
| "I want to be a dermatologist" / any specialty pre-claim | ROAD-specialty fixation pre-application reads as status-driven |
| "Clinician" (when describing herself) | Strict reading = licensed independent practitioner. MA does not qualify. Overclaim risk. |
| "Passionate about" anything | Generic; signals weak voice |
| "Hands-on experience" | Cliché; the role description IS the hands-on experience |
| "I've always wanted to help people" | The single weakest opener in modern admissions |
| "Innovative AI tool" / "AI-powered" / "AI-driven" | 2025–2026 these phrases now scan as marketing copy / "tech bro" |
| "Disruptive" / "transformative" / "revolutionary" | Same |
| Tagline / motto / brand statement | Triggers "this is a personal brand, not a person" read |
| Posted GPA / MCAT score | Even if strong, posting publicly signals insecurity |
| Photo of self with patients (even de-identified) | HIPAA / professionalism risk |

**Action for the site:** scan the current copy for any of these. Already-removed in the last edit pass: tagline, "builder," generic AI line. Verify the remaining copy is clean.

Add (or extend):
