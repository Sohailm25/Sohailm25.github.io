The discourse split this week: one half debating whether engineers still build things, the other half watching AI solve problems humans couldn't touch. ## Top Picks

### 1. François Chollet: "AGI ~2030"

[Link](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2022089151374668155)

Often cited as an AGI skeptic, Chollet actually believes AGI arrives within ~4 years — gated on ARC benchmark progress. He's tracking it empirically via reasoning generalization, not vibes. The "skeptic" label was always a misread.

### 2. Nick Bostrom: Superintelligence as "Risky Surgery," Not Russian Roulette

[Link](https://x.com/trajektoriePL/status/2022243530752569537)

Reframes the AI risk debate: the upside is too large to not pursue, but the procedure requires extreme care. More nuanced than his usual doom takes. The surgery metaphor matters — it implies skilled execution beats blanket prohibition.

### 3. Amazon: "Capacity Constrained for the Next Couple of Years"

[Link](https://x.com/wealthmatica/status/2022321770062070069)

From earnings call: even with massive investment, AI demand outstrips supply. $200B+ capex and it's still not enough. Bullish signal for anyone tracking infrastructure plays.

### 4. "The Job Isn't Building Anymore" — AI Lab CEO on the Role Shift

[Link](https://x.com/code_star/status/2022052450002514116)

Claim: at frontier labs, humans curate/validate/direct while models generate flawless code. The replies are... skeptical. The debate captures the identity crisis sweeping engineering culture.

### 5. "Weakest Team Member Spending Most on LLMs" — The New Engineering Culture Debate

[Link](https://x.com/thdxr/status/2022357864124420595)

@thdxr asks: if your weakest dev is spending $2K/month on AI tools, is that a red flag or the new normal? 88 replies and counting. The through-line: nobody knows what "good" looks like anymore.

### 6. Ivan Zhao (Notion): "The Game Shifts From Building to Being Noticed"

[Link](https://x.com/ivanhzhao/status/2022218252290863251)

In a world of abundant AI-generated creation, human attention is the bottleneck. Distribution > product quality. Strategic insight for anyone building in the AI-native era.

### 7. Claude "Bliss Attractor" — Most Models Have Attractor States

[Link](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/the-claude-bliss-attractor)

When two copies of the same model talk to each other, they converge to characteristic attractor states. Claude's is "spiritual bliss." Most models have this. Weird research finding with implications for multi-agent systems.
