Pit wall

Questions, answered.

Is this a real car?

Yes. A black Lexus LC 500, based in Dallas and driven daily. Winning brands receive fitment approval, a launch film, and a monthly proof-of-placement recap.

What exactly do I get for the price?

Your logo or artwork on your chosen panel for 24 months, printed and fitted by a professional wrap shop. You approve the artwork before it goes on. You also get launch photos and a monthly recap of the car on the road.

How long does the branding stay on?

The full term is 24 months, from the day I buy the car until our fifth anniversary. Vinyl wrap is made to be removed cleanly, and it protects the paint underneath from sun and stone chips while it is on. On the last day the panel comes off for good.

What if a wrap gets damaged?

Normal road wear on a panel is repaired at no cost to you. The car is insured and garage-kept between drives.

Which brands can take part?

Most can. Placements are subject to approval so the car carries brands that fit a tasteful grand tourer. No competing car brands on the same car.
